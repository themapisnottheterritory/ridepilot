# Drivers, tablets and logins

**Driver can't sign in to the tablet**: open the driver (**Drivers** in the top menu), click **Reset tablet password**, and confirm. The new password shows on the screen; give it to the driver. Nothing is emailed (driver email addresses in RidePilot are placeholders that can't receive mail).

**Driver availability**: a driver can only be put on runs within their availability. Open the driver, and in **Recurring Availability** make sure the days and hours cover the runs (for example Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM).

**Office staff forgot their RidePilot password**: use "Forgot your password?" on the login page. The email comes from ridepilot@trn.gcrpc.org. Staff with outside email addresses (for example goliadcountytx.gov, co.lavaca.tx.us, outlook.com) should check Junk or quarantine and mark it "Not junk". An administrator can also set a new password: open the user and click **Reset Password**.

**Microsoft sign-in**: GCRPC staff can sign in with their Microsoft 365 account; the RidePilot password still works as a fallback.
