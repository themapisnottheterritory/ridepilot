# Vehicles: taking a bus out of service, disposition

**Out for a while (repairs, inspection)**: open the bus on **Vehicles**, click **Inactivate** and give the dates and a reason. Dispatch can't put it on a run between those dates, and it comes back by itself afterwards. Garages and moving a bus between yards are under Runs.

**Leaving the fleet (disposition)**: open the bus and click **Move to disposition**. Pick the date it comes out of service and add a note if you like (where it's parked, who's handling it). From that date dispatch can't put it on a run; if it is still on upcoming runs RidePilot says how many, so dispatch can give them another bus. It stays on the **Vehicles** list with a gold **Disposition** badge until the disposal is recorded.

**When it's gone**: on the bus's page click **Record disposal** and fill in how it left the fleet (Sold, Auctioned, Transferred to another agency, Traded in, Scrapped, Returned to lessor, Other), the date, the final odometer and any proceeds. The badge changes to **Disposed**. **Back in service** undoes either step if a bus was moved by mistake or is kept after all.

**Report**: **Reports > Vehicle Disposition** lists the buses in disposition now (with how many days each has been there) and the ones disposed of in a date range, with VIN, type, ownership, 5310, dates, method, final odometer and proceeds: the facts FTA asks for when a grant-funded vehicle leaves the fleet. Run it as Excel or PDF.

**Vehicle Summary report (revenue and non-revenue hours and miles)**: **Reports > Vehicle Summary**. Pick the dates, and one bus or all. For each bus it shows total, revenue and non-revenue hours and miles, trips and passengers. Revenue service runs from the first pick-up to the last drop-off; the trips from the garage and back are non-revenue. Hours come from the run's start and end on the driver's tablet, miles from the start and end odometer readings, and non-revenue miles are the road distance from the garage to the first stop and back. A run counts only when the driver has entered both odometer readings, started and closed out the run on the tablet, and marked at least one stop done; the report lists the runs that aren't, with what each is missing, so they can be fixed. Choose Excel, CSV or PDF to download it.
